> You have read a thousand entries. This one asks nothing of you. The tide is out, the boats rest on the sand, and the gulls have gone home. The light leaves the garden one windowsill at a time, and nothing anywhere needs deciding.

Run: `node main.ts` (writes `/output/entry.txt`, 230 characters).
